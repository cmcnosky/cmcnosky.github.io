BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A better first
conversation.

You do not need to choose a foam product before contacting Blue Star.

What should a homeowner include?+

Share the project location and describe what you are building or what is not working. Add plans, photos or an approximate surface area when available.

What should a builder or commercial team include?+

Send the current plans and any issued specifications, addenda, scope sheet, bid form or ordinary qualification requirements. Include the location and bid deadline.

Does uploading plans produce a firm quote?+

No. An upload starts a project review. A written proposal depends on current documents, measurements or quantities, site conditions, schedule and applicable project requirements.

Where does Blue Star work?+

Blue Star is based in Greer and lists South Carolina, North Carolina, Georgia and Tennessee as its service area. Coverage, travel and scheduling are confirmed for each project.
