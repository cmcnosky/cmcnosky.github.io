PRIVACY & PROJECT FILES

Know where
your information goes.

How this website handles your project information, files and review request.

Where your information goes

Our intake service stores your contact and project details in its database. Files are kept in private storage for authorized review.

The form accepts as many as five PDF, JPG or PNG files, up to 20 MB each. You can also submit an authorized shared-file link. Do not include passwords or access codes.

How we review a request

With your permission, ChatGPT (OpenAI) may process the details and files to prepare an internal brief. Blue Star checks the source material and makes the final recommendation.

The on-screen receipt confirms we received your request. We confirm prices, bids and appointments separately. Please call if you have a deadline.

What not to upload

Submit only material you are authorized to share for project review. Leave out account credentials, payment details and personal identifiers. If your documents require a controlled plan room or a nondisclosure agreement, call before uploading.

Details and requests

Read the intake service privacy and file information for the current handling details. For questions about a submission, email Joshua@bluestarsprayfoam.com or call (864) 373-4464.

This website

Outside the embedded intake form, this front end stores only the selected light or dark color preference in the browser. No marketing analytics or advertising tracker has been added to it.
