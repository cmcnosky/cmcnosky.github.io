SCOPE BEFORE ASSUMPTIONS

Price the surfaces.
Understand the conditions.

A proposal may need to account for surface area, specified system and thickness, access, substrate condition, preparation and required protective coatings. The building footprint alone is not a takeoff.

COMMERCIAL QUICK ANSWERS

Before you send.

Can I send only a bid invitation or scope sheet?+

Yes, as a starting point. Include the current drawings, specifications and addenda when they are available, along with the due date and contact person. Sending a package does not by itself confirm bid participation.

Can you provide our required insurance or qualification documents?+

Include the requirements and submission deadline with your invitation. Blue Star will confirm which documents it can supply before you rely on them for prequalification or award.

What could change the price between budget and bid?+

Common changes include revised spray areas or thickness, a different coating, extra mobilizations, lift access or removal of existing material. Ask which assumptions the budget carries and which items still need field verification.

Can Blue Star suggest an alternate?+

Blue Star can flag questions and discuss possible alternatives. Any change to the specified assembly still has to follow the project’s design, submittal and approval requirements.

Can the website issue a commercial budget?+

No. Blue Star first reviews the scope and available documents so any budget discussion has a stated quantity basis, assumptions and exclusions.

Do I need a complete drawing package to begin?+

No. A description, photos or rough surface area can start an early discussion. Blue Star will confirm what is needed before treating the request as a formal bid.
