05 / A direct way to evaluate fit

See how I work before you hire me.

I am not asking you to lower the bar. I am asking you to directly evaluate one capability the role requires.

Choose a short, time-boxed, hypothetical or already-resolved interview problem—not a live deliverable—and set the criteria that matter. Ask me to identify the real objective, surface the assumptions, explain what I would test, and state what evidence would change my conclusion.

If the result gives you a clearer problem definition or stronger support for the next decision, you have relevant evidence of fit. If it does not, you have learned that before making a hiring decision.

For substantive evaluation work, start with a paid, scoped review.
