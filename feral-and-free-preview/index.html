THE SECONDHAND DETAILS

Before you buy.

A few small checks make buying secondhand online much easier.

How do I know it’ll fit?

Look for the garment’s measurements, especially on vintage pieces. Lay something that fits you flat and compare it to the listing. If a measurement is missing, ask through that listing before ordering.

What should I look at besides the price?

Check the photos and condition notes for wear or flaws, then look at the total with shipping and any fees. Return terms can vary by marketplace or listing.

Can I save a find for later?

On eBay, use Add to Watchlist on an item or Save Seller on the shop. On Poshmark or Mercari, use Follow on the seller’s profile to find the shop again. Saving or following doesn’t reserve an item.
